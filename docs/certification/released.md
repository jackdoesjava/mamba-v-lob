# The released model mamba-130m-hf

Every number here is for `state-spaces/mamba-130m-hf`, 24 layers with `d_model = 768`, `d_inner = 1536` and `d_state = 16`, loaded from a local copy with transformers 5.15.1. The text is `data/text/public_domain_sample.txt`, 1024 tokens. The certified map is the float64 forward pass of `src/verification/released.py` without the float32 casts of transformers' slow path. With those casts reproduced the reference forward pass differs from transformers' float64 output by at most 0 on 1024 tokens, and without them by at most 2.58e-05, against a largest output of 67.29.

## The data-free certificate

The separated geometric bound `dt_hi sup|B u| / (1 - exp(-lam dt_lo))` needs the floor `dt_lo`, and is given as a base-10 logarithm because the floor underflows in float64.

## The local certificate and the attack

The perturbation is any point within `delta` in sup-norm of a layer's realised normalised input on the first L tokens of the text. The local certificate is the norm-propagation domain around that input, with the additive recurrence step and with the two steps of Corollary cor:local adapted to the Euler equilibrium `c = Psi(lam dt) B u / lam`: the running maximum `E_t = max(E_{t-1}, K_t)` and the recursion `E_t = K_t + a*_t (E_{t-1} - K_t)`, with `K_t = dc_t + (e^rho_t - 1)|h_{t-1} - c_t|`, and the endpoint form, the reported one. The endpoint form bounds the one-step error identity `e_t = s e_{t-1} + x~ (b - b~) + (Abar_t - s) h_{t-1} + (x - x~) b`, with `x = lam dt`, `b = B u / lam` and `s = exp(-x~)` the perturbed transition, by `s E_{t-1} + x~ db_t + |(Abar_t - s) h_{t-1} + (x - x~) b|` and takes its largest value over the interval that holds `s`, the intersection of the intervals given by `rho_t` and by the additive bound on `|dt - dt~|`. That value is at an end of the interval or at one stationary point in closed form, and it is never larger than the recursion or the additive step. It is computed at layers 0 to 23. The attack runs at layers 0 to 23 at L = 64; layers 0 to 23 at L = 256; layers 0, 6, 12, 18, 20, 23 at L = 1024. Per (layer, length) it ranks every (position, output coordinate) pair with 32 random sign probes, takes the exact gradient row of the best-ranked pairs (24 at L = 64, 24 at L = 256, 32 at L = 1024, plus 8 at the last position), and runs sign-gradient ascent for 12 steps from 4 restarts per `delta`: the signs of the two best rows, one random corner, and the best row at the last position, the last on the largest change at the last position. Every best point is scored again with the reference forward pass. Values are per unit `delta`: the largest change over positions and coordinates divided by `delta`.

## Scope

All bounds hold over the reals for the float64 map. The released model as run by transformers rounds the residual stream, RMSNorm, `A`, the timescale bias, `B`, `u` and `D` to float32, and nothing here bounds that rounding. The data-free certificate covers every input of a layer; the local certificate and the attack cover the sup-norm ball of radius `delta` around one realised normalised input, which is not restricted to RMSNorm outputs. The incremental certificates are per layer with respect to the normalised input and do not compose through the residual stream and the RMSNorm in front of the next layer. The text file states in its header that it was transcribed to ASCII, so dashes and quotation marks may differ from a printed edition.
